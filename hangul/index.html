Every Hangul syllable is one block: an initial consonant, a vowel, and sometimes a final consonant (받침, batchim). A syllable that starts with a vowel sound uses the silent ㅇ. Build the block that matches the sound.

Block shapes

Tall vowels (ㅏ ㅓ ㅣ) sit to the right of the consonant: 가. Wide vowels (ㅗ ㅜ ㅡ) sit below it: 고. A final consonant always goes at the bottom: 각, 곡.

Romanization follows the official Revised Romanization of Korean.
